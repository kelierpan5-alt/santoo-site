PAN YAO / 潘垚

Visual artist / Abstract painting
Active since 2010

Pan Yao is a Chinese visual artist whose practice has developed from line and body-responsive image-making through realist study toward abstract painting. The current Ming series explores density, balance, boundaries, color, and negative space.
