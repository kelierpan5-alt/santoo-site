Chai Song

Chai Song is an ink painter living and working in Japan.

Artist introduction

Chai Song is an ink painter living and working in Japan.

Through tonal variations of ink and the density of brushwork, he depicts trees, vegetation and the stillness of water. Painted forms and unpainted space create layers of depth and a quiet, poetic atmosphere.
