SANTOO / ART & CULTURAL DIALOGUE

SANTOO

SANTOO is an artist collaboration initiative started by Pan Yao, bringing artists together to engage with cultures around the world through international art projects.

About SANTOO

Cross-cultural artistic collaboration, initiated by Pan Yao.

SANTOO brings together artists with different creative backgrounds. Through participation in international art projects, it seeks dialogue and shared creation with local cultures, lived experiences and artistic communities.

Starting from painting and work across media, the initiative explores exhibitions, collaborative creation and local exchange while respecting each artist’s independent perspective.

The initiator’s studio practice developed through Wenzhe Wujiang Studio, established in 2010, and Santoo Studio, established in 2018. Structural Studies continues as a research strand within SANTOO.

Artists

Pan Yao

Initiator of SANTOO

Pan Yao is a Chinese visual artist working primarily in abstract painting. Active since 2010, his practice has developed from line and body-responsive image-making through realist study toward abstraction.

Chang Bin

Chang Bin is an independent artist working across abstract painting, watercolor, and oil painting. He graduated from Handan University in 2003 and completed the oil-painting degree-completion program at the China Academy of Art in 2006.

Chai Song

Chai Song is an ink painter living and working in Japan.
