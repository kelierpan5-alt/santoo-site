01 / Position

Fiction / Poetic Prose / Long-Duration Narrative

Writing as an Extended Practice

Pan Yao's writing moves between fiction, poetic prose, and long-duration narrative projects. Classical and mythic structures are used as perceptual frameworks through which fracture, fatigue, suspension, and transformation can be examined.

Writing remains an independent creative practice while sharing concerns with the visual work: attention, duration, structure, and the limits of coherence.
