05 / Operational System

Methods

How does the system operate?

A cumulative method moving from attention dynamics to studio operation, methodological synthesis, and practical application: Attention → Method → Synthesis → Application.

01 / Attention

Research Model

ASAM

Adaptive Switching in Attention Allocation

ASAM models how attention dynamically redistributes within complex visual environments, stabilizing perception under symbolic density.

02 / Method

Studio Operation

Ming Working Method

A practice-based working method for maintaining visual consistency, organizing observations, and clarifying decisions during artistic production.

Ming remains an internal operational term; it is not presented as a doctrine or standardized scientific method.

03 / Synthesis

Methodological Synthesis

Structural Cognition Model

A developing methodological synthesis connecting ASAM, studio observation, visual analysis, and interdisciplinary writing without treating artistic judgment as a standardized measurement system.

04 / Application

Applied Systems

Applied Systems

Current applications include abstract painting, visual analysis, structural writing, sound composition, temporal structure, auditory attention, and interdisciplinary theory production.
