02 / Art Projects

Pan Yao × Chang Bin

Contemporary Painting, Art History, and Classical Images

The collaboration explores structure, looking, and the reinterpretation of images in contemporary painting. Pan Yao begins with the abstract paintings and viewing experiments of the Ming series; Chang Bin brings his abstract work, art-historical research, and watercolor interpretations of classical images into the dialogue.

The artists retain their individual bodies of work while exploring how composition, color, material, and space shape the experience of looking, and how painting traditions can be understood anew through contemporary practice.

26 April 2026 / Beijing

Both artists participated in an exhibition and symposium organized by the Beijing Abstract Symbolism Art Salon, presenting their respective works and discussing structure and artistic perception.

03 / Japan / Collaboration

Creative Practice, Exhibitions, and Local Exchange

I hope to gradually develop opportunities for artistic production, exhibitions, and professional exchange in Japan, making dialogue with the local art context part of my continuing practice.

I welcome conversations with arts organizations, exhibition spaces, residency programs, curators, and artists in Japan about workspace, presenting work, research exchange, and access to local resources.
